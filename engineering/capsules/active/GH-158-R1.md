# GH-158-R1 — Resume task capsule history consolidation with complete workflow authority

## Goal

Resume GitHub issue #158 from the cancelled GH-158 predecessor and complete the
transition from retained per-task terminal capsules to one detailed,
mechanically validated `engineering/capsules/HISTORY.md`.

The corrected scope explicitly includes `engineering/workflow/WORKFLOW.md`,
whose live Complete gate was the authority omitted from GH-158.

## Outcome

`engineering/capsules/HISTORY.md` is the durable current-tree authority for
terminal task outcomes, while full schema-v1 capsules exist only for active
execution.

The original 69 pre-migration terminal tasks remain represented exactly once.
Cancelled predecessor GH-158 is added as the 70th terminal history record with
its exact final full-capsule recovery locator and SHA-256 before the legacy
`engineering/capsules/completed/` tree is removed.

After implementation, GH-158-R1 remains the sole active task until normal
verification, review, integration, and terminal closeout. Its own closeout then
adds the next unique history record and removes its active capsule without
recreating `completed/`.

## Non-goals

- Rewriting, filtering, squashing, garbage-collecting, or otherwise altering Git history.
- Reducing historical `.git` object storage.
- Weakening active task planning, READY qualification, verification, review, or integration.
- Changing application runtime behavior, APIs, persistence, schemas, migrations, dependencies,
  toolchains, release metadata, or GitHub Actions.
- Moving product, architecture, or operational authority into task history.
- Reproducing every Goal, Background, Non-goal, and Escalation paragraph from every historical
  capsule when the exact full capsule remains recoverable from Git.

## Background

GH-158 established the initial 69-record consolidated history at exact commit
`cb40b610dc9355b9c990db9c9cff7284fb854ce1`. That checkpoint proved 69 unique IDs, 69 resolvable
historical full-capsule locators, and 69 matching SHA-256 bindings.

Execution then discovered that `engineering/workflow/WORKFLOW.md` still owned a
live instruction to move completed capsules to `completed/`, but that file was
outside GH-158's qualified scope. The task stopped rather than expanding scope
after READY.

GH-158 was formally cancelled at `31a3c7c3c1e0478537161eac41213fae6f4a21d1`. Its authorized partial implementation
was preserved externally with SHA-256 `6daa9e5169eca3503f4d38af12f847b4d441077a5474ca2112bb1ab534c96d4f`. GH-158-R1 resumes the same
issue with the missing workflow authority explicitly included.

  captured by the preflight inventory.

  repository-state change.

## Decisions and assumptions

- The history document may remain detailed; eliminating current-tree duplication is more important
  than minimizing the index.
- Active task capsule schema version 1 remains unchanged. `HISTORY.md` is a separate durable-history
  format.
- The original 69 migrated records retain their recovery locators at exact historical commit
  `8fead7f4eefd0a494ecab55dc6a13463f4169494`.
- Cancelled GH-158 uses its final cancellation checkpoint `31a3c7c3c1e0478537161eac41213fae6f4a21d1` and
  `engineering/capsules/completed/GH-158.md` as its full-capsule recovery locator.
- Future tasks use an exact commit/path where the full capsule still exists immediately before
  terminal closeout removes it.
- Terminal outcome authority moves to `HISTORY.md`; terminal tasks do not retain full capsules in
  the current tree.
- A later retrospective updates the existing unique history entry rather than recreating a capsule.
- The `completed/` directory has no permanent role after this migration and must not be recreated.
- GH-158-R1 remains active through review and integration. Its own terminal closeout is the first
  closeout performed entirely under the new history-plus-active-removal procedure.
- The preserved GH-158 partial patch is evidence, not authority. It may be reapplied only where it
  remains consistent with this corrected capsule and all workflow authority.
- Workflow-policy tasks must inspect `engineering/workflow/WORKFLOW.md` before READY; that omission
  caused the predecessor cancellation.
- No material assumption remains unresolved.
