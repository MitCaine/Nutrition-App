# E4-10 — Add the four-card History overview and daily mini charts

## Goal

Turn the E4-09 History route shell into the compact multi-day overview required by the frozen Epic 4 product contract without creating another nutrition-calculation, target, navigation, or runtime authority.

## Outcome

When a selected History range contains Logs, History shows exactly four stable Calories, Protein, Carbohydrate, and Fat cards. Each card reports the selected denominator's average and usable-day count, optional current target/reference context, and one discrete daily mini chart containing all 7 or 30 calendar observations. Selecting a chart date reveals exact date/value state without immediate Daily Log navigation. `Show more nutrition` transitions within the existing History navigation session to a distinct Nutrition Details shell reserved for E4-11.

## Non-goals

- No full nutrient catalog on the History overview.
- No E4-11 grouped Nutrition Details implementation.
- No E4-12 focused nutrient History implementation.
- No target/reference line on overview mini charts.
- No historical target versioning.
- No percentages, trends, prior-period comparisons, coaching, streaks, or pass/fail judgments.
- No weekly aggregation, dropped dates, or changed meaning for 30-day mode.
- No backend, API, runtime, SQLite, migration, or cache-identity change.
- No dedicated chart-accessibility redesign beyond ordinary operable controls and factual labels; dedicated chart accessibility remains deferred by the frozen Epic 4 decisions.

## Background

E4-09 is merged and archived. It owns the Daily-Log-owned History route, exact 7/30 LocalDate range model, yesterday cap, whole-period paging, authority-global `firstLoggedDate` retention, Complete/Logged denominator selection, authoritative-calendar launch gate, exact-range query lifecycle, and AppNavigator-owned `HistorySession`.

E4-05 already projects one E4-04 range payload into canonical nutrient rows containing the selected-denominator average, nutrient-specific usable-day count, one per-date projected state, exact numeric daily amount when usable, explicit-zero identity, and separate `gap` versus `unavailable` states.

The mobile package already includes `react-native-svg` 15.15.4. E4-10 may use it for rendering only; historical arithmetic remains owned by E4-05.

Current target/reference context is a separate current lens. Existing `useTargetConfiguration()` exposes current `effectiveTargets`; E4-10 must not request one historical target comparison per date or place target context into the History range query key.
