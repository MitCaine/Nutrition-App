# GH-132-P2R1 — Use one universal blue selected-navigation treatment

## Goal

Replace feature-specific selected-navigation coloration with one universal blue
selection treatment while preserving the navigation's existing geometry,
interaction, accessibility, and non-color selected cues.

## Outcome

Foods, Daily Log, and Recipes all use exactly the same selected-navigation
treatment:

- selected fill: `dailyLogBackground`;
- selected icon: `dailyLogForeground`;
- selected label: `dailyLogForeground`;
- selected clipped edge: `dailyLogForeground`;
- selected divider or dividers: `dailyLogForeground`.

Inactive navigation remains neutral and unchanged.

The P1 semantic category colors remain available but are not consumed by bottom
navigation. They are reserved for later surfaces where multiple information
types coexist and color materially improves differentiation.

## Non-goals

- No feature-specific Foods teal or Recipes violet in navigation.
- No change to P1 palette values.
- No `AppTheme.tsx` change.
- No primary-action color change.
- No inactive-navigation recoloring.
- No screen, card, header, History, or chart coloration.
- No geometry, dock-contour, safe-area, routing, or touch-target change.
- No backend, persistence, runtime, package, or CI change.

## Background

GH-132-P2 mechanically implemented Foods teal, Daily Log blue, and Recipes
violet/plum in bottom navigation. Mandatory physical review confirmed that the
implementation rendered correctly but rejected the semantic model.

The navigation already identifies the selected destination through its selected
third, clipped edge/dividers, filled icon, bold label, and accessibility
selected state. Assigning different hues to destinations therefore added visual
distinction without useful additional information.

The approved correction is to use the P2 Daily Log blue treatment universally
for selected navigation and reserve semantic category colors for co-present
information within later screens.

GH-132-P2 was cancelled and archived without merging its rejected navigation
implementation. Exact successor base is
`dc97e3985f3ecf3980e15accdd94221fde38288a`.
