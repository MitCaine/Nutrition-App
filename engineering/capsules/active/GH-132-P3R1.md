# GH-132-P3R1 — Strengthen History selected-date affordance

## Goal

Replace P3's physically undersized circular selected-date marker with a stronger
phone-scale selection treatment while preserving the semantic-series work that
passed native physical qualification.

## Outcome

The accepted P3 overview mapping remains unchanged:

- Calories → `nutritionCaloriesSeries`;
- Protein → `nutritionProteinSeries`;
- Carbohydrate → `nutritionCarbohydrateSeries`;
- Fat → `nutritionFatSeries`.

Selected numeric and explicit-zero bars use two simultaneous cues:

- their fill changes to the existing History selection color supplied from the
  theme, restoring the stronger selected-bar recoloring approved by the human
  owner;
- a 2-pixel outline uses the bar's original `barColor`, keeping its nutrient
  series visibly identifiable.

Selected gap and unavailable dates use a short horizontal baseline dash:

- 16 pixels wide;
- 3 pixels thick;
- rounded line caps;
- centered in the selected date slot near the existing baseline;
- drawn with the same existing selection color.

The 3-pixel-radius circular marker is removed.

Focused arbitrary-nutrient History remains generic `theme.colors.accent` and
uses the same stronger selection treatment.

No palette, nutrition data, History model, target semantics, chart scale,
scrolling, slot geometry, reference-line behavior, selected-day reporting, or
accessibility-selected state changes.

## Non-goals

- No change to any P1 semantic or nutrition-series palette value.
- No change to P3's four-series mapping.
- No whole-screen History identity color.
- No red/green or other nutrition adherence judgment.
- No semantic-class coloring of arbitrary focused nutrients.
- No large rectangle around a selected date slot.
- No chart scale, baseline, viewport, scrolling, slot-width, or touch-target
  redesign.
- No backend, persistence, API, navigation, package, or CI change.
- No hard-coded production color.

## Background

GH-132-P3 mechanically passed all seven acceptance criteria. Native physical
review in both light and dark appearance accepted the Calories, Protein,
Carbohydrate, and Fat semantic-series differentiation, focused arbitrary
nutrient behavior, 7-day and 30-day behavior, and existing chart semantics.

Physical review rejected only the generalized circular selected-date marker.
Its 3-pixel radius was judged too small/subtle at physical-phone scale.

The human owner explicitly confirmed that selected-bar color change was
acceptable. P3R1 therefore restores that stronger visual emphasis while adding
an original-series outline so selected numeric state is not communicated by
fill hue alone. Gap and unavailable slots receive a substantially larger but
still restrained baseline dash.

P3 was cancelled rather than regressed because the correction changes its
post-READY contract. P3R1 deliberately inherits the accepted P3 production
tree from exact cancellation checkpoint `60f457afef0fffa3c848fca8c15795ee18790dfc`.
