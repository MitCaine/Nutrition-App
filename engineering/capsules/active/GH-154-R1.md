## Background

uncommitted candidate. During its WF-003 package, the specified
active-to-completed move caused GH-154 capsule validation to fail because
`planning_artifacts` still required the WF-003 active path.

That was a post-READY authority change. Repository workflow rules therefore
prohibited repairing the authority in place, and the state machine provided no
`IN_PROGRESS -> DECOMPOSED` transition. GH-154 was terminally cancelled at
commit `467936f5ac11df36c67b4d944f7e5f13eef8cf67`.

SHA-256 is `e7b108e0c97140255db5ac23a097331e0277e0d946ef0b95fb996c0e8d222fee`.

The successor must establish corrected authority first. All four mutable

WF-003 completed — are execution scope rather than `planning_artifacts`
authority. Stable planning authority comes from workflow contracts, the
