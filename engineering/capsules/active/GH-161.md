# GH-161 — Retire Epic 5 and reorganize the future-product register

## Goal

Remove current-documentation drift that still presents the original Recipe Reuse and Discovery
outcomes as unfinished Epic 5 work and that mixes implemented product behavior with genuinely
deferred personal-product and scalability ideas.

## Outcome

Current project authority describes Recipe Reuse and Discovery as outcome complete and retired as a
planning unit, the current Recipe guide explains the delivered reuse/discovery/logging/duplication
experience, and the future-product register cleanly separates personal-app evolution from
scalability/multi-user/commercial architecture without presenting implemented History or
accessibility behavior as absent future functionality.

## Non-goals

- No Recipe, History, accessibility, OCR, target, account, synchronization, cloud, or analytics implementation.
- No retroactive Epic 5 Grill, PRD, architecture package, or implementation backlog.
- No Recipe-favorites authority, ranking authority, duplication-lineage persistence, or compatibility-projection redesign.
- No rewriting of historical Version 1.1 planning or terminal capsule history.
- No promotion of future-register ideas into committed roadmap work.
- No workflow/schema, CI, dependency, release, migration, persistence, API, or application-runtime change.

## Background

The post-GH-159 repository audit identified two related documentation defects. Current state and
roadmap authority still say Epic 5 is planned and requires re-scope even though its remaining
bounded outcomes were deliberately delivered by GH-149 and GH-150. Separately,
`docs/project/future-product-and-scale.md` correctly says implemented behavior should be removed or
narrowed, but still describes portions of the implemented History and accessibility experience as
future scope and does not clearly separate personal-product ideas from scale/commercial concerns.

Discovery at the frozen base confirmed that the stale Epic 5 strings are also mechanically enforced
by `scripts/validate-docs.py`, with focused validator ownership in
`apps/backend/tests/test_project_audit.py`. The current Recipe guide already documents Recipe
authoring, immutable publication, revision-backed logging, compatibility projections, and History,
but it does not yet consolidate the shipped GH-149/GH-150 product experience into a clear
Recipe-oriented current-behavior section.

## Decisions and assumptions

- Epic 5 is outcome complete and retired as a planning unit; it will not receive a retroactive
  Grill/PRD/backlog merely to reproduce an obsolete planning structure.
- GH-149 owns the final first-class Recipe reuse/discovery presentation work, and GH-150 owns
  independent Recipe duplication; their terminal records remain the bounded delivery evidence.
- Existing publication, immutable revision, serving/yield, nested-Recipe, logging, and projection
  architecture counts as already-delivered prerequisite/core behavior rather than new GH-161 work.
- The current Recipe guide may retain compatibility-projection architecture for maintainers, but
  the newly consolidated current-product description will use Recipe-oriented concepts.
- The future register is an idea register. Reclassification does not authorize implementation.
- Personal-app ideas are those with standalone value for the existing single-user local-first
  product; scalability/commercial ideas are those whose complexity is primarily justified by
  accounts, multiple users/devices, hosted operation, collaboration, or commercial operations.
- Commercial telemetry belongs in the scalability bucket; non-clinical personal trends and
  adherence-style summaries belong in personal-product evolution.
- Cloud backup remains distinct from synchronization even when both are classified as scale-facing
  future options.
- Historical material remains immutable point-in-time provenance.
