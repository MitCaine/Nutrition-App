# GH-132-P4R3 — Clarify selected History crossing caret

## Goal

Correct the selected-state contrast failure found during GH-132-P4R2 physical
qualification without changing its accepted unselected treatment or any
P4R1/P4 geometry and semantics.

## Outcome

P4R2 remains unchanged for unselected strict crossings:

- outer caret stroke is `barColor`, width 4;
- inner caret stroke is `selectionColor`, width 2.

For a selected strict crossing only:

- outer caret stroke remains `barColor`, width 4;
- inner caret stroke becomes `referenceLineColor`, width 2.

Both layers retain identical P4R1 coordinates and rounded caps.

This leaves a neutral visible center inside the selected caret's blue outer
stroke, separating the marker from both the pale selected-bar fill and the
blue selected-bar outline.

No new theme token, color prop, hard-coded color, or target/adherence meaning
is introduced.

## Non-goals

- No change to unselected P4R2 rendering.
- No change to crossing eligibility.
- No change to caret coordinates, 12-pixel width, 7-pixel height, or bar-top
  anchor.
- No change to selected bar fill, outline, or stroke width.
- No change to reference-line rendering.
- No change to exact above-reference visible or accessibility text.
- No target, theme, model, projection, backend, API, navigation, package,
  persistence, or History-screen change.

## Background

P4R2 solved the original caret contrast problem on ordinary blue bars.

Native review found one remaining state-specific ambiguity. When a crossing bar
is selected, the bar changes to `selectionColor` fill with a `barColor`
outline. P4R2's caret also uses `barColor` outside and `selectionColor` inside.
The inner caret therefore merges into the selected fill, leaving a blue outline
that is visually too similar to the selected bar's own blue outline.

The correction changes only the selected caret's inner presentation layer.
