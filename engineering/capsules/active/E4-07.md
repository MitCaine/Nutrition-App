# E4-07 — Reorder Daily Log around logging and add Complete control

## Goal

Restore Daily Log as the fastest logging surface while exposing authoritative Complete state, History navigation, and compact daily nutrition without adding another tab or reopening established runtime semantics.

## Outcome

Daily Log presents its selected date, a sticky Complete action, Previous/History/Next navigation, a compact four-nutrient summary, meal groups and Add Food in logging-first order, and a View Nutrition action. Complete reflects authoritative state and reuses the existing E4-02/E4-03 deterministic recovery contract.

## Non-goals

- No History charts, range UI, denominator controls, or drill-down; E4-09 owns the History surface.
- No exhaustive Daily Nutrition implementation; E4-08 owns that surface.
- No fourth bottom tab.
- No manual un-completion.
- No persistence migration, new runtime capability, synchronization, fallback, or authority mixing.
- No broad redesign of target authority or tracking semantics.

## Background

E4-01 through E4-06 are integrated. E4-02 already owns deterministic Complete mutation/reconciliation and E4-03 owns atomic invalidation after nutrition-changing Log mutations.

Current Daily Log places full Target Progress and Totals before meal logging. `RootScreenHeader` has title plus Settings only. `DailyLogsRuntime.markDayComplete()` exists, and the recovery journal already persists exact Complete payloads and reconciles/retries them.

The selected-date Daily Summary currently exposes nutrient totals but not Complete state. The remote Complete service already exposes owner/date `get_completion()`, and local SQLite already exposes `readLocalDailyLogCompletion()`. E4-07 therefore adds `is_complete` to the existing Daily Summary projection rather than creating another endpoint or changing the bounded History contract.
