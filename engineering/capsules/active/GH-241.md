# GH-241 — Enforce minimum qualification profiles from changed paths

## Goal

Ensure an authorized candidate cannot under-select baseline checks for paths that require backend, mobile, PostgreSQL, or iOS-native qualification.

## Outcome

The controller rejects missing minimum profiles before dispatch or integration, while retaining exact owner-selected profiles and task-specific checks above the floor.

## Non-goals

- No silent addition of profiles to an existing owner authorization or capsule.
- No application, migration, qualification workflow, or dedicated-App transport changes.

## Background

`required_profiles_for_paths()` currently covers only the iOS-native floor. Ordinary backend, mobile, and database-authority paths can receive a narrower selected set despite unchanged exact-profile binding. The current iOS floor itself covers `scripts/lib/task_authorization.py`; this issue carries `profile:ios-native`. Per owner instruction, run that expensive check only once at the end on the final candidate after the capsule and other checks are stable. The earlier candidate d8b33e3e9f4ac24a6456093481c2ec55891f1454 and provisional review remain preserved at the older base; no qualification or approval is inferred from them.
