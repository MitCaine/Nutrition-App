# GH-132-P4R1 — Anchor History crossing caret to bar top

## Goal

Correct the single physical-placement failure found during GH-132-P4
qualification while preserving all accepted P4 reference-crossing semantics.

## Outcome

Every numeric History bar whose exact value is strictly greater than the
current valid reference retains the P4 neutral upward caret.

The caret is no longer vertically centered on the reference line. Its upward
apex is anchored exactly to that bar's rendered top edge.

For the existing 12-by-7-pixel caret:

- horizontal center remains the center of the existing 44-point date slot;
- horizontal endpoints remain 6 pixels to either side of center;
- the apex Y equals the rendered numeric bar's top Y exactly;
- both outer endpoint Y values equal the apex Y plus 7 pixels;
- stroke remains 2 pixels;
- line caps remain rounded;
- color remains the existing `referenceLineColor`;
- rendering remains after the bars.

This intentionally places the caret body immediately inside the top edge of the
column while its apex touches the column boundary. The column therefore becomes
the visual anchor for the crossing indicator.

## Non-goals

- No change to which values qualify as strict crossings.
- No selected-only crossing rule; selected and unselected crossing bars use the
  same caret-placement rule.
- No change to the caret width, height, stroke width, line caps, or color.
- No change to reference-line position, width, color, or semantics.
- No change to bar value, rendered height, width, fill, outline, baseline,
  scale, viewport, scrolling, date-slot width, or touch target.
- No change to P4 exact `above reference` visible or accessibility text.
- No change to P3R1 selected numeric, explicit-zero, gap, or unavailable
  treatment.
- No target, theme, History model, projection, nutrition, API, backend,
  navigation, package, or persistence change.

## Background

GH-132-P4 mechanically qualified all seven acceptance criteria. Native physical
review accepted the strict-crossing rule, neutral styling, exact delta text,
reference treatment, and existing chart behavior.

Physical review rejected only the caret's vertical anchoring. P4 centers the
caret on the reference line. When a column top is close to that line, the caret
can appear detached from the column rather than visually belonging to it.

The human owner approved a bounded correction: keep the arrow as the
above-reference indicator but make its tip touch the column top so the
indicator is anchored to an existing visual boundary.

## Decisions and assumptions

- P4R1 preserves the P4 rule that every strict crossing receives the cue;
  selection state does not determine whether a caret exists.
- The user's requested arrow `tip` is the upward caret apex, defined as the
  minimum SVG Y coordinate of the two line segments.
- The visual anchor is the exact top boundary of the already-rendered numeric
  `Rect`.
- In SVG coordinates, the caret apex equals `Rect.y`; its two outer endpoints
  are 7 pixels lower, at `Rect.y + 7`.
- The 12-pixel width, 7-pixel height, 2-pixel rounded stroke, and existing
  neutral reference-line color remain frozen.
- P4's reference line still expresses the threshold. P4R1 changes only where
  the crossing symbol is drawn, not what crossing means.
- No unresolved implementation assumption remains at READY.
