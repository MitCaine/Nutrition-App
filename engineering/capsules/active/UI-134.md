# UI-134 — Make Recipe Details task-first and keep header compact

## Goal

Make Recipe Details use the same compact route-chrome hierarchy established by Food Details: fixed chrome owns route identity and primary route actions, while arbitrary Recipe identity and contextual/destructive content remain scrollable.

## Outcome

Recipe Details uses one standard-height fixed header containing Back, generic `Recipe`, Publish or Republish, and Edit. The complete Recipe name moves to naturally wrapping scrollable body content. Publish/Republish no longer requires scrolling through long ingredient and nutrition sections, while all existing publication eligibility, idempotency, mutation, ownership, logging, nutrition, and deletion semantics remain unchanged.

## Non-goals

- No Recipe persistence, publication model, API, runtime, SQLite, migration, or immutable revision change.
- No Recipe-to-published-Food authority change.
- No publication eligibility redesign.
- No idempotency or retry-identity redesign.
- No ingredient, yield, nutrition-resolution, logging, or deletion redesign.
- No shared route-header primitive redesign.
- No arbitrary Recipe-name truncation.
- No horizontal title-reveal gesture.
- No E4-14 Food nutrient-discovery work.
- No unrelated Recipe authoring redesign.

## Background

Native iPhone review after UI-133 exposed the same fixed-chrome issue on Recipe Details.

The current screen renders the full `recipe.name` as the `RouteScreenHeader` title. Long names therefore increase the fixed header height.

The primary Publish/Republish control is currently rendered in the ScrollView only after Ingredients, Total Recipe nutrition, Per Serving nutrition, Per 100 g nutrition, and publication/error messaging. Recipes with many ingredients therefore require substantial scrolling to reach a primary route-level action.

The screen already owns the correct authorities. `canPublishRecipe` decides publication eligibility. `publishRequestRef` plus `createClientRequestId` preserve request identity. `publish()` owns the existing mutation and published-Food navigation. `editBlockedMessage` owns Edit availability. UI-134 must move presentation around these authorities rather than replacing them.

## Authority and precedence

1. GitHub #134 owns the accepted Recipe Details interaction.
2. Existing `canPublishRecipe` remains authoritative for Publish/Republish eligibility.
3. Existing `publish()` and `publishRequestRef` remain authoritative for mutation and idempotency behavior.
4. Existing `editBlockedMessage` behavior remains authoritative for Edit availability.
5. Existing Recipe nutrition, logging, published-Food, and deletion paths remain authoritative.
6. Existing `RouteScreenHeader` and `RouteHeaderAction` primitives remain unchanged.

## Allowed changes

- Change Recipe Details fixed route title from `recipe.name` to generic `Recipe`.
- Render the complete Recipe name at the beginning of ordinary scrollable content.
- Add Publish/Republish to the existing route-header trailing region.
- Keep Edit in that same trailing region.
- Use existing `RouteHeaderAction` treatment for both primary route actions.
- Label the publish action `Publish` when unpublished and `Republish` when a published Food exists.
- Pass existing publication disabled/busy state directly into the header action.
- Invoke the existing `publish` function directly from the header action.
- Remove the former scroll-body Publish as Food / Republish Food button.
- Keep publication requirement/error/status/republish messaging in the body.
- Keep View Published Food and Log Recipe in the body.
- Keep Delete Recipe in the body.
- Update directly affected rendered-screen and route-layout tests.

## Forbidden changes

- Do not change `canPublishRecipe`.
- Do not change publication request ID generation or `publishRequestRef` semantics.
- Do not change the publish mutation payload.
- Do not change published-Food navigation after successful publication.
- Do not change Recipe API types/hooks/mutations.
- Do not change Recipe ownership, revision, or publication authority.
- Do not change Recipe nutrition-resolution helpers.
- Do not change logging authority.
- Do not change deletion or dependency-removal semantics.
- Do not modify shared `RouteScreenHeader` or `RouteHeaderAction`.
- Do not truncate or horizontally scroll the complete Recipe name.
- Do not move View Published Food, Log Recipe, or Delete Recipe into sticky chrome.
- Do not change backend/runtime/SQLite/migrations.
- Do not expand into Recipe authoring or E4-14 Food authoring.
