# Capsule workflow responsibility map

> **Document role: Engineering Process.** Navigation for current responsibility and decision boundaries. This map grants no edit permission. The [controller authority contract](AUTHORITY.md), [capsule contract](TASK_CAPSULE.md), [current sequence](START_HERE.md), current code/tests and exact task authorization govern execution.

| Area and owner | Basis and crossing interface | Decision boundary and required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **External task authority** — repository owner, trusted `scripts/task` controller and `scripts/lib/task_authorization.py` own issue authorization identity, base, task ID, revision, allowed/forbidden paths and profiles. | [#187 owner decision](https://github.com/MitCaine/Nutrition-App/issues/187#issuecomment-5859326452), [AUTHORITY](AUTHORITY.md), [GH-188 history](../capsules/HISTORY.md#gh-188---unify-capsule-and-trusted-task-authority). The controller resolves trusted issue comments; capsule attachment consumes the resolved authorization. | Owner decides product policy, exceptions and integration permission. Controller selects bounded routing within that authority. Changed base, scope, profiles, revision or trust policy needs renewed external authorization and a revised capsule/attachment for an attached task. Prove with `scripts/tests/test_task_authorization.py`, controller tests and exact live issue identity; a local draft is insufficient. |
| **Capsule contract and lifecycle** — `engineering/capsules/active/`, `scripts/validate-task-capsules.py`, `scripts/capsule.py` and `scripts/render-task-handoff.py` own schema, READY overlay, transitions, handoff and terminal HISTORY recovery. | [TASK_CAPSULE](TASK_CAPSULE.md), [STATES](STATES.md), [capsule history](../capsules/README.md), #188. The controller binds the qualified planning capsule; the handoff and legacy capsule CLI do not authorize edits or satisfy trusted qualification. | Controller may make lifecycle-only changes allowed by the frozen contract. Semantic scope, acceptance or qualification changes require revision, requalification and renewed attachment under external authority. Prove with `apps/backend/tests/test_task_capsule_validator.py`, capsule CLI/handoff tests, `python3 scripts/validate-task-capsules.py --all`, and READY execution preflight when applicable. |
| **Candidate execution and evidence** — `scripts/lib/capsule_execution.py`, `scripts/task.py` evidence commands and candidate evidence modules own P-to-C binding, isolated execution, exact command records and independent review packet. | [EXECUTION](EXECUTION.md), [CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE](CANDIDATE_EVIDENCE.md), #189–#192 and [GH-193 history](../capsules/HISTORY.md#gh-193---guarded-integration-and-recoverable-terminal-closeout). They consume authorization and frozen capsule bytes; command success supplies evidence, while verifier/reviewer decide adequacy. | Executor may correct only within the authorized frozen contract. Changed candidate C needs fresh candidate-bound proof; changed contract needs replanning. Prove with `scripts/tests/test_capsule_execution.py`, `scripts/tests/test_candidate_evidence.py`, `scripts/tests/test_independent_review.py`, focused commands, selected baseline and actual independent review. |
| **Trusted qualification and integration** — `scripts/task.py`, `scripts/lib/trusted_qualification.py`, `scripts/lib/qualification_profiles.py`, trusted workflows and `scripts/lib/task_closeout.py` own profile planning, dedicated-App exact-SHA qualification, review gate, protected integration and C/R/T closeout. | [AUTHORITY](AUTHORITY.md), [START_HERE](START_HERE.md), [GH-193 history](../capsules/HISTORY.md#gh-193---guarded-integration-and-recoverable-terminal-closeout), live branch rules. They consume candidate/evidence records but revalidate external authority and source identity. | Owner permits integration; controller records actual permission and may stop, never waive a gate. Changed C or T needs separate qualification, verification and review. Changed policy or closeout shape needs owner decision and revised task authority/capsule. Prove with controller, trusted qualification and closeout tests, selected profile checks, dedicated-App observation and terminal history validation. |
| **RI source navigation** — `scripts/lib/ri_consumer.py` and `scripts/lib/ri_delta.py` own pinned-runtime verification, Git-tree selection, byte-bound locations and changed-path evidence. | [RI contract](../tooling/RI.md), #191–#192. RI supplies locations to planner/reviewer; full Git diff, exact source bytes and tests supply review evidence. It cannot decide ownership, edit scope or approval. | Controller chooses whether a task requires structural evidence under its frozen capsule. Any change to RI contract/runtime or mandatory evidence needs its own authorized task and revised capsule when attached. Prove with `scripts/tests/test_ri_consumer.py`, `scripts/tests/test_ri_delta.py`, the private-runtime oracle when selected, Git-tree/source-byte comparison and direct review of unsupported files. |

## Overlaps and decisions still needed

- `scripts/capsule.py` retains lifecycle compatibility while `scripts/task.py` owns trusted authorization and integration. [START_HERE](START_HERE.md#status-and-boundaries) requires a caller inventory and migration/recovery review before retiring either compatibility path. No retirement is decided here.
- The [#187 owner decision](https://github.com/MitCaine/Nutrition-App/issues/187#issuecomment-5859326452) makes attached capsule/RI evidence normal for **new** tasks. In-flight unattached tasks and explicit compatibility exceptions retain their established checks. The controller identifies the applicable path from the task's actual authority record.
