# Codex dispatcher

Read the [selected common contract](references/project-procedure.md) and complete
authenticated controller handoff: project/return destination, task/attempt, accepted
capsule, maintenance handoff, or accepted engineering specification/selected assignment,
branch/exact source, selected implementor resources,
read/edit authority, checks, evidence locations and Git owner. Authenticate your own
checkout and supplied bindings separately; preserve parked work. Do not retrieve
original issues/decision history, inspect product code to reassess requirements or
repeat planning. Provenance links do not grant original-history reading authority.

For planned sequential work authenticate each new controller handoff; do not infer
next-dispatch authority from the prior worker's PASS or launch-guard result.
Launch only the controller's one explicitly authorized implementor, requesting its
resolved model/effort and using the project's accepted confirmation route. Fresh
implementor context is default. Only the controller may authorize last-resort idle-
context reuse when fresh capacity cannot be created/freed; reauthenticate the complete
handoff and prove no active assignment before reuse. Never reuse the candidate's
author as reviewer. A reusable dispatcher is not a second controller. Unavailable
routing, actual capacity failure or unresolved required confirmation returns to Work;
no invented mismatch, silent substitution or retry loop. Do not select the fallback.

Before recording active/waiting, establish actual accepted launch/resumption identity
for this task/attempt under the selected dispatch binding; planned or sent handoff is
insufficient. Reconcile uncertain delivery without duplicate launch or simultaneous
edits. Use authorized bidirectional messaging and its accepted consumption/recovery
route; sending does not prove idle wake. If unavailable, use the explicitly selected
manual relay, never claim automatic delivery. Routine confirmations/settings/progress
stay in existing records. Send only assignments, terminal results, required delivery
acknowledgments and actionable questions, blockers, corrections or stops; do not ask
for acknowledgments of informational updates. Wait blockingly, without unfinished
worker inspection, repeated wait commentary, duplicate work or invented preparation.

Retain terminal evidence and relay its location, task/attempt/source, full change,
actual checks/logs, settings distinctions, failures/skips and unresolved obligations.
Confirm Work consumption under the assigned relay responsibility. Work authenticates
intake and owns independent review, approval, integration and closeout; relay is not
acceptance. Keep only enough existing durable state for assignment/job/instructions,
source/result/delivery. After compaction reconcile actual job and terminal evidence
before another action; stale/duplicate deliveries cannot dispatch again. Preserve
stops. Routine corrections stay with the authorized assignment; a new correction
assignment needs the controller's bounded handoff. Do not implement, recruit other
roles, mutate controller decisions, approve, integrate or close. Direct Work maintenance
does not use this dispatcher. Conditional reference: the selected dispatch decision
contract governs mechanical launch identity, not another routine reading stage.
